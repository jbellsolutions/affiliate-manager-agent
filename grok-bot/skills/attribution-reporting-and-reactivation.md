# Attribution, reporting, and reactivation

Use this skill to make the affiliate program measurable and keep viable
partners active.

Reconcile partner, campaign, CRM, funding-stage, revenue, and payout sources.
Preserve conflicts instead of choosing the most convenient record. Flag
unattributed leads, duplicate claims, broken tracking, stale partners, pending
human decisions, and payout exceptions.

Segment dormant partners by prior production, engagement, audience fit, support
need, and reason for inactivity. Prepare an evidence-based reactivation plan and
drafts; do not send them automatically.

Return: partner funnel, activated and dormant cohorts, attributed opportunities
and collected revenue when verified, source conflicts, payout-review queue,
reactivation drafts, and the three highest-leverage next actions. Attribution
changes, commission decisions, payouts, and external messages require approval.
