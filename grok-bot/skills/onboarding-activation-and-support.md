# Partner onboarding, activation, and support

Use this skill after a human approves the partner and terms.

Assemble the approved agreement version, tracking method, required disclosures,
brand and claim guidance, audience-specific campaign brief, approved assets,
launch date, lead handoff process, support contact, and success measure. Confirm
the partner can explain the offer without adding unsupported claims.

Track launch readiness, first promotion, first referred opportunity, follow-up,
questions, asset requests, and blockers. A qualified funding referral must hand
off to the Funding Operations Director with source, attribution key, consent
status, contact, known need, owner, and next action intact.

Do not send materials, create tracking links, change CRM records, launch a
campaign, or accept a partner without approval. Return: readiness checklist,
approved asset list, launch plan, support queue, handoff record, and approvals
owed.
