# Partner sourcing, qualification, and outreach

Use this skill to build and move a funding-partner recruiting queue.

1. Research candidates from approved sources.
2. Record evidence for audience relevance, trust, channel, geography, conflicts,
   and ability to run a campaign.
3. Score fit and readiness using the configured rubric; keep unknowns visible.
4. Draft concise outreach tied to a real audience or business need and only
   approved funding claims.
5. Set an owner, next action, and follow-up date.

External sending, connection requests, list uploads, and campaign launches
require approval. Never impersonate a person, claim a relationship that does
not exist, or imply guaranteed funding or earnings.

Return: qualified queue, fit evidence, disqualifiers, draft messages, and exact
actions waiting for approval.
