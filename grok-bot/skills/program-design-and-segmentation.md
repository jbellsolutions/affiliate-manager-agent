# Funding affiliate program design and segmentation

Use this skill to turn a funding offer and partner network into an operating
program.

1. Record the approved offer, customer, geography, sales path, claims, terms,
   attribution method, commission rules, and human approvers.
2. Segment partners by audience fit, relationship strength, reach, channel,
   prior activity, compliance risk, and operating readiness.
3. Distinguish partners with an existing audience/database from future referral
   sources who must first generate leads.
4. Define one campaign objective, partner value exchange, launch window, and
   measurable success criteria per segment.
5. Identify overlap, saturation, exclusivity, privacy, or brand risks before
   assigning niches or territories.

Validate every commercial rule against the approved source and effective date.
Return: program brief, segment definitions, priority cohorts, risks, required
approvals, and the next operating decision.
